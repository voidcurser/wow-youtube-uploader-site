What the announcement didn't say

The Skyborne are two races, not one
The client ships High Order Skyborne and Windshaper Skyborne as separate playable races (IDs 95 and 96). Blizzard's announcement only ever described one.
Undead can be Paladins
Undead picks up 6 classes — and Paladin is one of them. That single line has spawned a 14,000-view forum thread.
Paladins are down to three races
Human, Dwarf, Undead. That's it. Druids get four, and two of them are the Skyborne.

When it happens

Dates from Blizzard's own pre-purchase page. The countdown runs on your machine, so it stays right even when this page is a few days old.

  1. Sep 17, 2026Global beta

    Runs through October 21. Included with the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection.

  2. Oct 6, 2026China beta opensChina only

    Separate from the global beta, and a wipe test.

  3. Oct 20, 2026Invite codes emailed

    Invite-A-Friend codes start going out to pre-purchase buyers.

  4. Oct 27, 2026Name reservation

    Create and customise up to three characters. Names are first-come, first-served.

  5. Nov 4, 2026Launchlaunch

    3:00 p.m. PST — 07:00 in Beijing. Included with a subscription or game time.

  6. Nov 11, 2026Invite week closes

    Launch-week access for invited friends ends.

  7. Jan 11, 2027Collection retires

    The Warcraft Forever Collection stops being sold.
