# Generic WoW thumbnails

The recording popup now has a **Game** selector. **WoW Retail** offers the existing
Mythic+ and general-video flows. **WoW Forever** uses its separate Journey to 60
template, character list, remembered choices, description and hashtags; see
`../Forever/README.md`.

`Stonewarden.png` is the approved reusable Stonewarden artwork. The source image
is never changed or deleted when an upload completes.

Use **Other WoW video…** beneath Upload and Skip to upload general gameplay.
Choose a character, enter a video title, and optionally add a description and
specialization. WoW hashtags and character details are added automatically,
without Mythic+ tags. New videos upload as public in both upload modes.

For Stonewarden, the headline appears in the open space on the right. Leave
**Thumbnail headline** blank to use the video title, or enter a shorter headline.
Headlines allow up to **60 characters including spaces**, wrap onto at most three
lines, and never shrink below 44 pixels on the 1280 × 720 output. Very wide words
may need shortening even within the character limit. Around 20–45 characters is
a useful target; there is no minimum length. Use **Preview thumbnail** to check.
The full YouTube title may contain up to 100 characters.

Characters without a generic template upload without a custom thumbnail.
Additional approved templates go in this folder as `<CharacterName>.png` and
should reserve the same headline area on the right. A different character's
artwork is never substituted.

As with Mythic+ uploads, completed uploads remove the local MP4 and generated
thumbnail. Failures retain local files; thumbnail retries use the existing video
ID instead of uploading the video again. Restart the uploader to load changes.
