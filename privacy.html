Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. About the Application

WoW YouTube Uploader is a personal desktop application that automates uploading World of Warcraft gameplay recordings to YouTube.

2. Google Account Access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to request authorization to upload videos to the user's YouTube channel.

Authentication tokens are stored locally on the user's computer and are used to maintain authorized access to YouTube.

3. Information Used

The application processes locally stored gameplay recordings, video titles, character information, and generated thumbnails.

Authorized YouTube access is used to upload videos and their associated metadata and thumbnails.

4. Data Storage and Sharing

Application settings and authentication credentials are stored locally on the user's computer.

Video recordings, thumbnails, and associated metadata are transmitted to YouTube when the user authorizes an upload.

The application does not operate an independent server for collecting personal information.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Local recordings and generated thumbnails may be deleted automatically after successful upload, according to the application's configured workflow.

Users can revoke the application's Google Account access through their Google Account settings.

6. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the application developer using the support email provided on the Google OAuth consent screen.
